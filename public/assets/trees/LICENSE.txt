Tree assets and algorithm credits
=================================

The procedural tree generator (src/world/treeGen.ts) is a TypeScript port of the branching
algorithm from ez-tree (@dgreenheck/ez-tree 1.1.0), and the leaf cards in this folder are derived
from its leaf textures (*_leaf.png: recoloured, edge-cleaned, and for cypress/gorse/birch modified).
