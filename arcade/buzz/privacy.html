Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This explains what Buzz collects, why, and what you can do about it. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account: your email (to send sign-in codes), your name and username, and that you confirmed you're 18 or older.
  • Your area: the school or location you pick, so Tonight shows parties near you. If you use “Use my location”, your phone's location is used once to set that area.
  • Parties: the parties you request, join, pay for, RSVP to or check in to. For hosts: your orgs, the parties you post (including the address and flyer), and your guest lists.
  • Crowd votes: your Dead / Chill / Packed vote, kept for 6 hours. If you vote from nearby, your location is checked against the party once and not stored.
  • Chat: messages you post in your area's chat, deleted after 48 hours.
  • Friends and safety: your friends and requests, people you block, and reports you make.
  • Notifications: your notifications (kept 30 days) and, if you turn them on, your phone's push token.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We keep a record of each payment (amount, fees, refunds) for accounting.
  • Technical logs: basic server logs used to fix problems, kept for 14 days.

Who can see what

  • Hosts see the name and username of people on their guest list, and their status (requested, paid, checked in). They don't see your email.
  • Your friends see your name on parties you're going to. Nobody else does.
  • People in your area's chat see your messages with your name and username.
  • A party's exact address is only shown to approved guests and hosts.
  • Buzz admins can see reports and the content that was reported.

Services we use

Amazon Web Services (hosting, sign-in and email), Stripe (payments), Expo (push notifications) and Apple (the App Store). They process data only to provide those services to us.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in Profile → Delete account. That deletes your profile, username, friends, blocks, notifications, RSVPs, chat messages and sign-in, and removes your name from guest lists. Payment records are kept without your name because the law and hosts' accounting need them. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it deleted.

Age

Buzz is only for people 18 and older. If you believe someone younger is using it, tell us and we'll remove the account.

Changes

We'll update this page and the date above if anything changes, and the app will ask you to agree again for big changes.
