Something's wrong at a party or in the app

If anyone is in danger, call 911 first. To report a person, party or chat message, open it and tap ··· → Report, or long-press a chat message. You can block anyone from their menu. We review reports, usually within 24 hours.

Common questions

I didn't get my sign-in code. Check your spam folder and wait a minute, then go back and send it again.

Why can't I see the address? Hosts share the exact address only with approved guests. Request to join and you'll see it once the host lets you in.

How do I get a refund? If the host cancels, you're refunded automatically. Otherwise ask the host, who can refund you from their guest list.

How do I change my school or location? Profile → tap your area.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Delete account, at the bottom.
