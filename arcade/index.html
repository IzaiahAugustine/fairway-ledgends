Questions

Frequently asked

Are the games free?

Yes. Fairway Legends and Kitchen Clash are completely free, including online matches in Fairway Legends and every mode in Kitchen Clash.

Do I need to download anything or make an account?

No. Both games run in your web browser: open the page and you're playing. There are no accounts.

What devices can I play on?

Fairway Legends works on computers, phones and tablets. Kitchen Clash plays on a computer with a keyboard or game controller, and on phones and tablets with on-screen controls (turn your phone sideways). Any recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari works, and both games adjust their graphics to keep things smooth.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, in your browser on the device you play on. Fairway Legends keeps your level, XP, clubs and golfer; Kitchen Clash keeps your settings and favorite modes.

Can I play with friends?

In Fairway Legends, choose Play Online, create a match and send your friend the invite link. In Kitchen Clash, choose Play Online, create a match and send your friend the code or invite link to play 1 on 1. You can also team up with an AI partner in doubles.

How do I play Kitchen Clash?

Start with How to Play on the title screen: a quick hands-on lesson. Move with WASD and hit with J (drive), K (soft) and L (power), or use the arrow keys with Z, X and C. On a phone, drag the left side of the screen to move and tap HIT, SOFT or POWER. The same buttons turn into dinks, drops, lobs and smashes depending on where you are on the court.
