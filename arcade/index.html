Questions

Frequently asked

Is Fairway Legends free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play, including online matches against friends.

Do I need to download anything or make an account?

No. It runs in your web browser: open the page and you're on the first tee. There are no accounts.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, in your browser on the device you play on. Your level, XP, clubs and golfer are there next time you visit.

How do I play against my friends?

Choose Play Online, create a match and send your friend the invite link. You take turns from your own computers and see each other's shots live.

Can I play on my phone or tablet?

The game is built for a computer with a mouse right now. A mobile version with touch controls is planned, followed by apps for iPhone and Android.

What computer do I need?

Any recent laptop or desktop with Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. The game adjusts its graphics automatically to keep things smooth, and you can choose Low, Medium or High quality in Settings.
